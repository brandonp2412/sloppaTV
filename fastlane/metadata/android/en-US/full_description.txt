sloppaTV is an independent Android TV client for Jellyfin media servers. Connect to a Jellyfin server you control and watch your library with a remote-first TV interface.

Browse home sections, movies, shows, collections, genres and people. Search your library, open detailed media information, resume where you left off, and manage watched or favourite status from the TV.

Playback adapts to your server and device. sloppaTV supports Jellyfin Direct Play, Direct Stream and server transcoding, with audio and subtitle selection, queues, autoplay, seeking, playback controls, refresh-rate handling and HDR capability negotiation where supported by the device and media.

You can save multiple Jellyfin users and servers, switch profiles, use Quick Connect, tune playback and subtitle settings, and use Android TV search and media controls.

A Jellyfin server and account are required. Media availability, codec support and whether a file can play directly depend on your Jellyfin server, media and Android TV device.

sloppaTV is an independent client and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Jellyfin project.
