sloppaTV is an independent Jellyfin client built for Android TV and Google TV. Enjoy movies and shows from your own Jellyfin media server with a clean, remote-first interface.

EXPLORE YOUR LIBRARY
• Browse movies, TV series, collections, genres and people.
• Search your library, view detailed information and continue watching where you left off.
• Mark titles watched, manage favourites and switch between saved users.

PLAYBACK BUILT FOR TV
• Direct Play, Direct Stream and server transcoding, depending on your setup.
• Choose audio tracks and subtitles, seek, queue episodes and use autoplay.
• Refresh-rate handling and HDR capability negotiation when supported by the device and media.

EASY SERVER ACCESS
• Sign in to a Jellyfin server you control, including Quick Connect.
• Save multiple Jellyfin servers and users and customise playback and subtitle settings.
• Use Android TV search and media controls.

PRIVACY
No ads, third-party analytics SDKs or developer-operated backend. sloppaTV connects directly to the Jellyfin server you choose. Your server's security and data policies determine how it handles your account and library.

WHAT YOU NEED
A Jellyfin media server and account are required; sloppaTV does not provide any media. Actual playback, codec, HDR and transcoding capabilities depend on your media, server and device.

sloppaTV is an independent client and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Jellyfin project.
